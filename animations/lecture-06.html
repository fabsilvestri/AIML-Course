Decision trees

CART, one split at a time. Every split you see is the result of scanning every threshold on every feature and keeping the one with the smallest weighted impurity — the scan is drawn too, under Gini and under entropy. Press Step to advance one unit and read what happened; press Play to let it run.

1CART, split by split

Ninety points of three classes in the plane. Each step takes the next node in breadth-first order and splits it with the question $x_k \le t_k$ that minimises the weighted impurity $J(k, t_k)$. Below the data, the scan that chose it: $J$ against every candidate threshold, on each feature, divided by the node's own impurity so that Gini and entropy share an axis. On the right, the tree so far; a left branch means the answer was yes.

The mathematics

With $p_k$ the share of class $k$ in a node,

$$\begin{aligned}G(\mathbf p) &= 1 - \sum_k p_k^2,\\ H(\mathbf p) &= -\sum_k p_k \log_2 p_k .\end{aligned}$$

A candidate split sends $m_{\text{L}}$ of the node's $m$ instances left and $m_{\text{R}}$ right, and costs

$$J(k, t_k) = \frac{m_{\text{L}}}{m}\,I_{\text{L}} + \frac{m_{\text{R}}}{m}\,I_{\text{R}}.$$

Candidates are the midpoints between adjacent distinct values of $x_k$ in the node. The scan plots $J / I_{\text{parent}}$: dividing by a number that is the same for every candidate changes no ranking, and puts both criteria on $[0, 1]$. The impurity decrease is $\Delta I = I_{\text{parent}} - J$.

Watch for
  • Every boundary is a horizontal or vertical segment, and every region is a rectangle: one question about one feature per node.
  • The root's scan runs over 82 candidates on each feature. Late splits are made on nodes of a dozen instances or fewer: the scan shrinks to a short, jagged curve, and the winner is decided by a handful of points.
  • The solid and dashed minima usually sit at the same threshold — Gini and entropy choosing the same split. The readout counts how often they did.
Try this
  1. Raise the maximum depth to 12 and play to the end. Training accuracy reaches 100%: nothing stopped it.
  2. Then set the minimum leaf size to 10. Fewer, larger leaves; the stray points in the wrong region are left alone.
  3. At depth 8, play to the end under each criterion and compare. The first 19 splits are identical; the two part at split 20, on a node of only 11 instances.
